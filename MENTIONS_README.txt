ICRP HUB MASS-MENTION POLICY

Only Founder, Co-Founder, Owner, Head Director, and Deputy Head Director
are allowed to use @everyone and @here.

All other ICRP roles have Mention @everyone, @here and All Roles disabled.
This includes moderators, staff, HR, platform members, and boosters.

Discord treats the @everyone and @here permissions as one permission:
mention_everyone. The same toggle also governs mentioning all roles.

WARNING: Any role with Administrator permission bypasses these restrictions.
A member with multiple roles can mention everyone if ANY role grants it.
Other non-ICRP roles (including third-party bot roles) must be audited manually.

New roles receive the policy with !setup_hub.
For EXISTING roles, server owner must run:
!apply_hub_permissions CONFIRM

This command updates the permissions of all mapped ICRP roles, not just mentions.
Review role_permissions.json and PERMISSIONS_README.txt first.
