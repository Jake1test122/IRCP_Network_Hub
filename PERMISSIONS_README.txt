ROLE PERMISSIONS
Admin: Founder, Co-Founder, Owner only.
Lead: Corporate, Assistant Founder, Co-Owner, Head Director, Deputy Head Director (management permissions, NOT Administrator).
Moderator: moderation and security roles (no Administrator).
HR: Human Resources roles (message management, no Administrator).
Staff: directors and staff (standard staff permissions).
Member: all other roles including accepted platforms and boosters (no elevated privileges).

New roles receive their tier permissions on creation.
Existing roles remain unchanged unless the server OWNER runs:
!preview_hub_permissions
!apply_hub_permissions CONFIRM

HR and OFFICES categories are staff-only; setup syncs category channel permissions.
IMPORTANT: Discord role hierarchy still restricts bot edits. Put the setup bot role above managed ICRP roles.
This is a recommended default policy, not a transcription of Discord's original permission toggles.
Review role_permissions.json and your server's desired access before applying.
